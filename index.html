Your additions

FRS needs no license in the US. GMRS does. Power limits are the FCC ERP maximums for handhelds and mobiles. Frequencies off by a few kHz usually come from receiver drift or rounding, not a different channel. The Flipper labels bands using world-wide plans, so a label such as GSM900 can sit on something different in the US. The list above is a guide for the US and is not a full allocation chart.
